Threat Landscape

Common Attack Vectors & Psychological Hooks

Attackers do not break into systems directly; they trick individuals into opening the doors.

🎯

Spear Phishing

Carefully researched, individualized communication using your name, job title, and company specifics to build immediate trust.

📱

Smishing & Vishing

Scams conducted through SMS texts or phone calls impersonating IT technicians, banks, or delivery couriers demanding two-factor codes.

👔

Whaling (BEC)

Executive impersonation attacks targeting finance personnel to execute unauthorized wire transfers or disclose employee tax information.

🪝

Watering Hole

Compromising a trusted third-party website frequented by an organization's staff to silently deploy drive-by malware payloads.

Primary Psychological Triggers

Artificial Urgency ("Within 24 hours") Fear of Penalties ("Account Termination") Perceived Authority ("Message from the CEO") Greed & Curiosity ("Q3 Performance Bonus")

    Standard Operating Procedures

    01

    Inspect the Envelope Sender

    Never rely solely on display names. Expand the header to verify the actual domain name after the @ character.

    02

    Hover Over All Hyperlinks

    Before clicking any link, verify the real destination URL displayed in your browser status bar or preview popup.

    03

    Use Secondary Out-of-Band Verification

    If an email requests wire transfers or credential changes, reach out to the sender directly via an official phone number.

    04

    Enforce Hardware Multi-Factor Authentication

    Use security keys (FIDO2/WebAuthn) or authenticator apps rather than plain SMS text verification wherever possible.
